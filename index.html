Approach

How I Work

Everything I do is focused on behavior change because this is the only way we change as humans. When we are stuck or struggling, it's usually because we're doing something that doesn't work. Oof, that sounds harsh! Think about it this way: the only thing we can really control in life is our own behavior, so if the solution doesn't involve altering your approach then you're a much worse kind of stuck. Here are some of the values that inform how I work:

Authenticity

Whether I'm doing therapy, consultation, or training I bring my authentic self to the work — and I invite those I'm working with to do the same. The thread through all of the different types of work I do is helping others to connect to their own experience and what's important to them. This creates the context for profound changes in behavior — which is, in fact, the only way we change at all.

Rigor

I only utilize approaches that have been shown through extensive research to be effective. There are lots of things that might help. I'm committed to only offering things that we know do help. I ask a lot of my clients and in turn I bring an intense focus to every interaction.

Compassion

When we feel safe and let our guard down, we can be the best versions of ourselves. All human experiences are welcome in our work together. Whatever shows up for you will be met with kindness, understanding, and curiosity. I'll help you learn to meet yourself with the same qualities, which is one of the most powerful tools for behavior change.

Creativity

When something's not working, it takes creativity to find a new path forward. In our work I'll offer a fresh perspective on relating to stuckness — as an opportunity to courageously confront what's not working and get deeply curious about what could. I'll invite you to experiment with new behaviors, learn from what works (and what doesn't), and evaluate the impact of our work by the extent to which you're living your life in ways that matter to you.

Purpose

I will never ask you to do anything without a clear purpose. The work will be grounded in your values — the defining qualities of who you want to be, and how you want to show up in the world. Our behavior is shaped by so many influences, from our biology to our thoughts and emotions. Choosing to swim against the current and ground our behavior in our values, rather than letting it be dictated by our fears and whims, is how lives are transformed.

Measurement

We will use validated tools to measure the impact of our work and ensure you are seeing the change you are seeking. I'll ask you to fill out brief questionnaires throughout our work and I'll check in with you regularly to make sure we're on the right track.

Fit

Let's make sure it's a fit.

I work with a small number of clients at a time and take care to ensure we are well matched. I've found it's most helpful to set up a brief consultation call before committing to working together, and if I'm not the right fit I'll offer referrals to a trusted colleague.

My license allows me to provide therapy to clients who reside in California.

Looking for executive coaching instead?

Coaching and therapy are different services with different scopes, and I keep them separate on purpose. If you are a leader looking for performance and leadership work rather than clinical care, feel free to reach out and we can discuss whether that's a better fit.
